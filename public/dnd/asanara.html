Asanara Mossbrook

Paladin 6 (Oath of the Ancients) — Human

At a Glance

  1. Before Combat
    • Finished a Long Rest? You may reassign your 2 weapon masteries — currently Greatsword→Graze, Greatclub→Push.
  2. Start of Your Turn
    • Frightened? Roll 1d20 — on 10+, you're no longer Frightened (Indomitable Heart).
    • Mounted? Announce your steed's action: Dash, Disengage, or Dodge.
  3. Your Turn: Action
    • Attack resolved? Apply the mastery effect: Graze = damage on a miss, Push = shove 10 ft on a hit.
    • Bloodied (≤32 HP) and landed a hit? You may declare max damage — 1/Long Rest (Defiant Strike).
    • Rolling two-handed weapon damage? 1s and 2s count as 3s (Great Weapon Fighting).
  4. Your Turn: Bonus Action
    • Bonus action: touch-heal from your 30 HP pool — DOUBLED in Longbow form — or spend 5 to cure Poisoned (Lay on Hands).
    • Bonus action: teleport 30 ft, costs a 2nd-level slot (Misty Step).
  5. End of Your Turn
    • LAST CALL before your turn ends: announce the Arms' form — Greatsword (+1 atk/dmg), Greatclub (+2 AC), or Longbow (ranged, 2x Lay on Hands)?
  6. Off-turn
    • Hit by an attack? Subtract 3 from Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing damage (Heavy Armor Master).
    • Damaged while concentrating? CON save, DC 10 or half the damage (whichever is higher) — you save at +5 in your aura.
    • When healed, add an extra +2 HP (Remarkable Recovery).
    • Allies within 10 ft of you get +3 on all saves (Aura of Protection).

Start of Your Turn

Indomitable Heart
Frightened? Roll a d20 — on 10+, the condition ends.
Mounted? Announce your steed's action
A controlled mount acts on your initiative and can ONLY Dash, Disengage, or Dodge. Pick one each round and say it out loud — its movement is how you get around.
📢 ANNOUNCE: Every round on a mount: Dash, Disengage, or Dodge?

Your Turn: Action

On hit/miss — weapon mastery
Every attack with a mastery weapon has a mastery effect — most trigger on a hit, Graze on a miss. Apply the mastery effect every time.
📢 ANNOUNCE: Attack resolved? Apply the mastery effect — hit or miss.
On hit — Defiant Strike (1/LR)
While bloodied (≤32 HP), after a hit lands you may declare the damage roll is maximized. Pairs devastatingly with a Divine Smite in the same hit.
📢 ANNOUNCE: Bloodied and the hit matters? Declare max damage now (1/Long Rest).
On hit — Great Weapon Fighting
Two-handed melee damage dice: 1s and 2s count as 3s.
Always available: Attack · Dash · Disengage · Dodge · Help · Ready · Shove / Grapple · Utilize

Your Turn: Bonus Action

Lay on Hands
Touch: heal any amount from your 30 HP pool, or spend 5 to cure Poisoned. The Arms' Longbow form DOUBLES the healing (Ebony Rejuvenation), and Remarkable Recovery adds +2 HP on top.
Misty Step
Teleport 30 ft (level-2 slot). Escapes grapples, Restrained-adjacent messes, and melee without Opportunity Attacks.

End of Your Turn

Arms of the Arcane Defender form swap
LAST CALL: choose next round's form. Greatsword = +1 attack & damage. Greatclub = +2 AC. Longbow = ranged (+2 dmg w/ Bracers of Archery) and DOUBLES Lay on Hands healing. The curse means this is your only weapon, so the form IS your weapon choice.
📢 ANNOUNCE: Say it before your turn ends: which form next round?
🔀 DECIDE: Hitting hard (sword), tanking (club), or ranged/healing (bow)?
