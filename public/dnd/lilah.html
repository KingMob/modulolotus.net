Lilah Silverlake

Rogue 6 (Inquisitive (XGtE)) — Human

At a Glance

  1. Start of Your Turn
    • Frightened? Roll 1d20 — on 10+, you're no longer Frightened (Indomitable Heart).
  2. Your Turn: Bonus Action
    • Not moving this turn? Get Advantage on your next attack — your Speed becomes 0 (Steady Aim).
    • No Advantage and no ally next to the target? Roll Insight against their Deception — unlock Sneak Attack against it for 1 minute if you win (Insightful Fighting).
    • Dash, Disengage, or Hide (Cunning Action).
    • Enemy hiding? Make a Perception check to spot it (Eye for Detail).
  3. Your Turn: Action
    • Have Advantage, or an ally within 5 ft of the target? Add 3d6 Sneak Attack damage to ONE attack this turn (Sneak Attack).
    • Attacking? The pistol shoots TWICE per Attack action, crits on 19–20, and ignores close-range Disadvantage (Ilseur's Hair Trigger / Acuity).
    • Dealing Sneak Attack damage? You may trade 1d6 of it for an effect: Poison (DC 13 Con), Trip (Prone), or Withdraw (Cunning Strike).
    • Did the pistol hit? Vex: Get Advantage on your next attack vs that target.
    • Bloodied (≤31 HP) and landed a hit? You may declare max damage — 1/Long Rest (Defiant Strike).
    • Need 10 temp HP? Spend the Tadpole's charge — returns on a Short Rest (Shield of Thralls).
  4. Off-turn
    • Hit by an attack from an attacker you can see? Reaction: HALVE the damage (Uncanny Dodge).
  5. Anytime
    • Bad d20 incoming? Spend a Luck Point (3/LR): get Advantage on your own D20 Test, or impose Disadvantage on an attack against you (Lucky).

Start of Your Turn

Indomitable Heart
Frightened? Roll a d20 — on 10+, the condition ends.

Your Turn: Bonus Action

Steady Aim
Grant yourself Advantage on your next attack roll this turn. Your speed becomes 0 until your next turn.
📢 ANNOUNCE: You will not move this turn (before or after the attack).
🔀 DECIDE: Worth it only if you don't need to move or reposition this turn.
Insightful Fighting
Your Wisdom (Insight) vs the target's Charisma (Deception). Win, and for 1 minute you can Sneak Attack that creature without Advantage or an adjacent ally (still not while you have Disadvantage). Lasts until you use it on someone else.
🔀 DECIDE: Your Sneak Attack unlock when positioning fails — you have Insight Expertise, so you usually win.
Cunning Action
Take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action as a bonus action instead of your action.
🔀 DECIDE: Disengage to leave melee safely; Dash to close distance; Hide to set up Sneak Attack.
Eye for Detail
Make a Wisdom (Perception) check to spot a hidden creature or object, or an Intelligence (Investigation) check to uncover a clue.

Your Turn: Action

Sneak Attack
Once per turn, add 3d6 extra damage to one attack with a Finesse or ranged weapon when you have Advantage, or when an ally is within 5 feet of the target and you don't have Disadvantage.
🔀 DECIDE: Pick the attack most likely to hit before applying it — it only triggers once per turn.
Ilseur's Prized Pistol
Attack action: shoot TWICE (Ilseur's Hair Trigger). +2 attack & damage, crit on 19–20, no Disadvantage for shooting within 5 ft of an enemy, no reloading. First shot hits? Vex gives the second shot Advantage — put Sneak Attack on that one.
🔀 DECIDE: First shot hits → second shot has Advantage (Vex) → Sneak Attack rides the second shot.
Cunning Strike
When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you can forgo 1d6 of it to add one effect: Poison — target makes a DC 13 Con save or is Poisoned for 1 minute (you have a Poisoner's Kit, required); Trip — Large or smaller target makes a DC 13 Dex save or falls Prone; Withdraw — after the attack, move half your Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
🔀 DECIDE: Trip early in the round so allies get melee Advantage; Withdraw when you're in someone's face.
On hit — weapon mastery
Every attack with a mastery weapon has a mastery effect. Pistol → Vex: after a hit, your next attack against that target has Advantage (until end of your next turn).
📢 ANNOUNCE: Did the pistol hit? Say it: next attack on that target has Advantage.
On hit — Defiant Strike (1/LR)
While bloodied (≤31 HP), after a hit lands you may declare the damage roll is maximized. Best spent on a hit that's carrying Sneak Attack dice.
📢 ANNOUNCE: Bloodied and the hit matters? Declare max damage now (1/Long Rest).
Shield of Thralls
Expend the Tadpole's charge to conjure a psionic shield — you gain 10 Temporary Hit Points. The charge returns on a Short or Long Rest.
Always available: Attack · Dash · Disengage · Dodge · Help · Ready · Shove / Grapple · Utilize

Off-turn

When you're hit
Uncanny Dodge: when an attacker you can see hits you with an attack, use your reaction to halve the attack's damage against you.
📢 ANNOUNCE: Say it the moment the damage is announced — before it's applied.
Always available: Opportunity Attack · Readied Action Trigger

Anytime

Luck Points
3 Luck Points per Long Rest. Spend 1 when you roll a D20 Test to give yourself Advantage, or when a creature attacks you to impose Disadvantage on its roll. Advantage from Lucky also unlocks Sneak Attack.
Always available: Interact with an Object
